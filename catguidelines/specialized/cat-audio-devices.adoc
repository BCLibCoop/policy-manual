Books On Audio Devices
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Books on audio devices 
Examples of books on audio devices are Playaways, Yotos, and Tonieboxes.

When cataloguing books on audio devices follow
these paramenters to ensure your items display as expected.

* Leader position 06 (Type) = i
* 008 position 23 (Form) = q

When these parameters are met a record will display in the staff and public 
catalogues with both the _Audio Device_ and 
_Preloaded Audio_ icons.
