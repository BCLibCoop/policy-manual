Books On Audio Devices
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Books on audio devices are audiobooks that are either preloaded on a proprietary 
audio device or preloaded onto a a card or figurine that is inserted into 
a proprietary audio device. Examples of books on audio devices are Playaways, Yoto cards 
and players, and Tonies and Tonieboxes.

Cataloguers should follow standard practices for cataloguing these items, with the exception of the 
specified parameters included in these guidelines.

When the specified parameters are met records for this type of item will display in the staff and public 
catalogues with both the _Audio Device_ and 
_Preloaded Audio_ icons.

Playaways
^^^^^^^^^

When cataloguing playaways follow these paramenters to ensure your playaways display as 
expected and do not appears as matches for standard books or audiobooks when records are 
loaded into Sitka's Evergreen.

* Leader position 06 (Type) = i
* 008 position 23 (Form) = q
* 020 (ISBN) $a = Used for the ISBN only if the ISBN is unique to the item
* 020 (ISBN) $z = Used for the ISBN if it the same ISBN belonging to the standard book
* 250 (Edition) = $aPlayaway

Yoto Cards and Player
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Yoto players should be 
https://docs.libraries.coop/sitka/_creating_new_records_for_non_bibliographic_resources.html[catalogued as non-bibliographic resources] 
per your library's policy on cataloguing equipment.

When catalogued separately from the player, Yoto cards should include the following specified 
parameters:

* Leader position 06 (Type) = i
* 008 position 23 (Form) = q
* 020 (ISBN) $a = Used for the ISBN only if the ISBN is unique to the item
* 020 (ISBN) $z = Used for the ISBN if it the same ISBN belonging to the standard book
* 250 (Edition) = $aYoto
* 538 (System Requirements) = $aFor use in Yoto Player
