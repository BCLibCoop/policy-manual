Audio-Enabled Devices
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Audio-enabled devices are audiobooks that are either preloaded on a proprietary 
audio device or preloaded onto a card or figurine that is inserted into 
a proprietary audio device. Examples of audio-enabled devices are Playaways, Yoto cards 
and players, and Tonies and Tonieboxes.

Cataloguers should follow standard practices for cataloguing these items, with the exception of the 
specified parameters included in these guidelines.

When the specified parameters are met records for Playaways, Yoto cards, and Tonies will display in the staff and public 
catalogues with both the _Audio Device_ and _Preloaded Audio_ icons.

image:images/guidelines-audio-devices-1.png[scaledwidth="75%"]


Yoto players and Tonieboxes should be catalogued as 
non-bibliographic resources per your library's policy on cataloguing equipment. They will display in the staff and public 
catalogues with the _Toy, puzzle, or equipment_ icon.

image:images/guidelines-audio-devices-2.png[scaledwidth="75%"]


Playaways
^^^^^^^^^

When cataloguing playaways include the following specified parameters:

* Leader position 06 (Type) = i
* 008 position 23 (Form) = q
* 020 (ISBN) $a = Used for the ISBN only if the ISBN is unique to the item
* 020 (ISBN) $z = Used for the ISBN if it the same ISBN belonging to the standard book
* 250 (Edition) = $aPlayaway

[CAUTION]
=========
It is very important that all 020 fields in the record are coded correctly. Incorrectly coded 020 fields may lead 
to playaway records being incorrectly merged with records for standard books and audiobooks.
=========
